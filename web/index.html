Alderwick

On the River Fenn, where the Elderwood keeps its own counsel.

Four families share this bend of the river. Bakers, wardens, ferryfolk and forgehands live by the clock of the sun — working, eating, gossiping and sleeping. Stay long enough, and you can learn the shape of every day. Choose who you are before you walk the gate.
